The shore

The tide brings you in with nothing.

Salt in your lungs, sand in your hair, a backpack and clothes worn to threads. Behind you, endless fog. Ahead, a mountain so tall its summit vanishes into the clouds, and the strange certainty that you are meant to climb it.

The island answers every step with something new: creatures that hunt in the mist, ruins that hum with old magic, and a strength inside you that grows with every fight you survive.

Every life counts

Time is the one thing you can't win back.

You grow older

Every hour you play, the years pass. You begin as a quick, fearless ten-year-old, grow into your full strength, then feel the weight of age as the climb gets harder. Each stage of life fights differently.

No saves

There is no reloading. Fall in battle, or let old age catch you before the summit, and the tide carries you back to the beach at ten years old. Your gear is gone. What you've learned is not.

You are not alone

Other travelers walk this island: real players, lost in the same fog. Trade what you've found, band together against what hunts in the mist, or settle things in a duel with a prize on the line.

Battle

Turn by turn, every choice matters.

Brush against a creature in the wild and the world snaps into battle. The quickest act first, and every hero in your party is a real player choosing their move. Your weapon decides your abilities, and gear attuned to the same element stacks into something devastating.

The island

Eight lands ring the mountain.

Every land is open from your first step. Nothing stops you from walking straight into danger except the danger itself. Each is ruled by a guardian: defeat it, and you'll walk away stronger, with your pick of rare gear forged for the road ahead.

Driftglass Shore

Wreck-strewn sands where the tide hums and the caves glitter with sea glass.

Fogwood Hollow

Whispering trees, and paths that rearrange themselves when you look away.

Saltmarsh Fen

Villages on stilts above a bog where strange lights drift after dark.

Whistling Bluffs

Cliffs carved by a singing wind, and bridges you'd rather not look down from.

Ember Caldera

The volcano's heart, where obsidian forges never cool.

Sunken Bell Ruins

A drowned city whose bells still toll beneath the water.

Frostveil Pass

A silent, frozen gateway at the mountain's foot.

Cloudline Scar

The final ascent, where the air thins and the clouds close in.

The Peak

No one has ever come back down to say what waits above the clouds.
